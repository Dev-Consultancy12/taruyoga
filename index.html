About Us

A calm Himalayan school for authentic yoga learning.

Welcome to Taru Yoga School, a place where traditional yogic wisdom meets modern learning in the peaceful surroundings of the Himalayas.

Located in the heart of Upper Bhagsu, Dharamsala, Himachal Pradesh, India, Taru Yoga School is dedicated to sharing authentic Yoga through Teacher Training Courses, Workshops and Daily Classes. Our mission is to help students deepen their understanding of Yoga as a way of life that brings balance, awareness and inner peace.
